# Changelog

## v1.1 (update for FeedPlay review)
- Social sign-up buttons (Google, Facebook, LinkedIn) in the LeadPal form, simulated in the prototype.
- Name field added; each lead records its source and the video time it was captured at.
- Form trigger is now a setting: 10%, 25% (default) or 50% of the video, instead of a fixed 10 seconds.
- New optional gate mode: viewers can skip the form. Required mode still pauses the video.
- Captured-leads table with an estimated usage fee at $0.09 per lead, as in the approved proposal.
- Export captured leads to CSV.
- "Replay from start" to demo the flow again.

## v1.0
- LeadPal email form over the FeedPlay player at 10 seconds; video resumes after sign-up.
